Kundalik — Maxfiylik siyosati

Ishlab chiquvchi: Moonquill Studio · Kuchga kirgan sana: 2026-yil 15-oktabr

Qisqasi: Kundalik sizning yozuvlaringizni hech qayerga yubormaydi. Ular faqat qurilmangizda, sizning parolingiz bilan shifrlangan holda saqlanadi. Biz ularni o‘qiy olmaymiz va ko‘ra olmaymiz.

Qanday ma’lumotlar saqlanadi

  • Kundalik yozuvlari, kayfiyat belgilari va teglar — faqat qurilmangizning o‘zida, AES-256 bilan shifrlangan holda.
  • Parolingiz hech qayerda saqlanmaydi. Undan faqat shifrlash kaliti hosil qilinadi.
  • Tiklash kodi ham saqlanmaydi — uni faqat siz bilasiz.

Biz nimalarni yig‘maymiz

  • Akkaunt, ism, telefon raqami yoki elektron pochta talab qilinmaydi.
  • Reklama, kuzatuv (tracking) va statistika (analytics) vositalari yo‘q.
  • Joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera yoki mikrofonga kirish so‘ralmaydi.
  • Hech qanday ma’lumot uchinchi tomonlarga berilmaydi yoki sotilmaydi.

Zaxira nusxalar

«Zaxira nusxa olish» tugmasini bossangiz, yozuvlaringiz shifrlangan faylga saqlanadi. Bu faylni qayerga yuborishni (masalan, Telegram yoki Google Drive) faqat siz tanlaysiz. Fayl ichida ochiq matn yo‘q — uni parolingiz yoki tiklash kodisiz ochib bo‘lmaydi.

Internetga ulanish

  • Google Play’dan o‘rnatilgan Android ilovasi ishlashi uchun internet talab qilmaydi va hech qanday serverga murojaat qilmaydi. Yangilanishlarni Google Play o‘zi yetkazadi.
  • Windows ilovasi va GitHub’dan o‘rnatilgan Android ilovasi yangi versiya bor-yo‘qligini bilish uchun vaqti-vaqti bilan GitHub (github.com) bilan bog‘lanadi. Bunda yozuvlaringiz yoki shaxsiy ma’lumotlaringiz yuborilmaydi. GitHub odatdagidek IP-manzilingizni o‘z qoidalariga ko‘ra qayd etishi mumkin.

Ma’lumotlarni o‘chirish

Barcha ma’lumotlar qurilmangizda bo‘lgani uchun ularni ilovani o‘chirib yoki ilova ma’lumotlarini tozalab, butunlay yo‘q qilishingiz mumkin. Bizning serverlarimizda o‘chirishga hech narsa yo‘q.

Bolalar

Ilova bolalarga mo‘ljallanmagan va hech kimdan, jumladan bolalardan ham, shaxsiy ma’lumot yig‘maydi.

O‘zgarishlar

Agar ilova qanday ishlashi o‘zgarsa (masalan, qurilmalar o‘rtasida sinxronlash qo‘shilsa), ushbu sahifa yangilanadi va yangi sana ko‘rsatiladi. Muhim o‘zgarishlar ilova ichida ham e’lon qilinadi.

Kundalik — Privacy Policy

Developer: Moonquill Studio · Effective date: October 15, 2026

In short: Kundalik never sends your entries anywhere. They are stored only on your device, encrypted with your password. We cannot read or access them.

What is stored

  • Diary entries, mood marks and tags — only on your device, encrypted with AES-256.
  • Your password is never stored; it is only used to derive the encryption key.
  • The recovery code is not stored either — only you know it.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account, name, phone number or email is required.
  • No advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.

Backups

When you tap “Back up”, your entries are saved to an encrypted file. You alone choose where to send it (for example, Telegram or Google Drive). The file contains no plain text and cannot be opened without your password or recovery code.

Internet access

  • The Android app installed from Google Play does not need the internet and does not contact any server. Updates are delivered by Google Play.
  • The Windows app and the Android app installed from GitHub periodically contact GitHub (github.com) to check for new versions. No entries or personal information are sent. GitHub may log your IP address under its own policies.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device, so you can delete it completely by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. There is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the way the app works changes (for example, if syncing between devices is added), this page will be updated with a new date. Important changes will also be announced inside the app.
